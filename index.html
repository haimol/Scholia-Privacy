Scholia Privacy Policy

Scholia for Mac and Scholia for iPad · Effective 27 October 2026

English中文

Scholia is a reader for research papers and the notes you write about them. It is made by an independent developer who runs no servers for it: Scholia does not send your papers, notes or reading to the developer, and the developer cannot see them.

What stays on your devices

Your library — PDFs, notes, highlights, saved translations, tags, reading positions and settings — is kept in a folder you choose on your Mac or iPad. Scholia also keeps simple reading statistics (such as pages read) inside your library, for your own use; they are not sent anywhere. API keys and the ChatGPT sign-in are stored encrypted on the device (in the macOS or iPadOS keychain) and are only ever sent to the service they belong to.

Syncing with iCloud

If you keep your library in iCloud Drive, or turn on Scholia’s iCloud sync, your library is stored in your own iCloud account (iCloud Drive or your private CloudKit database) so your Mac and iPad stay in step. Apple stores this data under its own terms and privacy policy; the developer has no access to it.

Services Scholia contacts, and only when you use them

Translation

When you translate text, or a paper with ScholiaSense, the text is sent to the translation service you chose in Scholia: ChatGPT (signed in with your OpenAI account and using your plan), the OpenAI API or another OpenAI-compatible service, DeepSeek, Anthropic, DeepL or Google Translate. With ScholiaSense, the paper’s paragraphs (and its title and any terms you set) are sent so they can be translated. With Ollama, translation runs on your own computer and nothing leaves it. Each service handles what it receives under its own privacy policy. Nothing is translated unless you ask for it.

Finding a paper’s details or PDF

When you add a paper by its identifier, look up its details, follow a citation, or look for a free PDF, Scholia sends the identifier or the paper’s title and authors to scholarly databases: Crossref, DataCite, OpenAlex, arXiv, doi.org, PubMed (NCBI), Europe PMC, Open Library or the Library of Congress. If a free PDF is found, it is downloaded from where it is published. Your own PDFs and notes are never sent.

Links you open

Web links you click open in your browser.

What Scholia does not do

  • No accounts with the developer, no advertising, no tracking, and no analytics or crash reports sent to the developer.
  • No selling or sharing of your information.

Your choices

You choose which translation service to use, or none. You can sign out of ChatGPT, remove API keys and clear saved translations in Scholia’s settings, and you can delete your library folder at any time. To stop iCloud syncing, turn it off in Scholia or move your library out of iCloud.

Children

Scholia is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: please open an issue on this page’s repository.

Scholia 隐私政策

Scholia（Mac 版与 iPad 版）· 2026 年 10 月 27 日起生效

Scholia 是一款用来阅读论文、并在论文旁写笔记的应用，由独立开发者制作。开发者没有为它运营任何服务器：Scholia 不会把你的论文、笔记或阅读内容发送给开发者，开发者也无法看到这些内容。

保存在你设备上的内容

你的文库——PDF、笔记、高亮、保存的译文、标签、阅读位置和设置——保存在你在 Mac 或 iPad 上选择的文件夹里。Scholia 还会在文库中记录一些简单的阅读统计（例如读过的页数），只供你自己查看，不会发送到任何地方。API 密钥和 ChatGPT 登录信息加密保存在设备上（macOS 或 iPadOS 的钥匙串中），只会发送给它们所属的服务。

通过 iCloud 同步

如果你把文库放在 iCloud 云盘里，或开启了 Scholia 的 iCloud 同步，文库会保存在你自己的 iCloud 账户中（iCloud 云盘或你的私有 CloudKit 数据库），让 Mac 和 iPad 保持一致。这些数据由 Apple 按其条款和隐私政策保存，开发者无法访问。

Scholia 会联系的服务（仅在你使用相应功能时）

翻译

当你翻译一段文字，或用 ScholiaSense 翻译整篇论文时，文字会发送到你在 Scholia 中选择的翻译服务：ChatGPT（使用你的 OpenAI 账户登录并使用你的套餐额度）、OpenAI API 或其他兼容 OpenAI 的服务、DeepSeek、Anthropic、DeepL 或 Google 翻译。使用 ScholiaSense 时，会发送论文的段落（以及论文标题和你设置的术语）以便翻译。使用 Ollama 时，翻译在你自己的电脑上完成，内容不会离开设备。各服务按其自身的隐私政策处理收到的内容。除非你主动要求，Scholia 不会翻译任何内容。

查找论文信息或 PDF

当你按标识符添加论文、查找论文信息、跟随引用或寻找免费 PDF 时，Scholia 会把标识符或论文的标题和作者发送给学术数据库：Crossref、DataCite、OpenAlex、arXiv、doi.org、PubMed（NCBI）、Europe PMC、Open Library 或美国国会图书馆。如果找到免费 PDF，会从其发布的位置下载。你自己的 PDF 和笔记永远不会被发送。

你打开的链接

你点击的网页链接会在浏览器中打开。

Scholia 不会做的事

  • 不需要注册开发者账户，没有广告，不追踪，也不会向开发者发送分析数据或崩溃报告。
  • 不出售、不分享你的信息。

你的选择

你可以选择使用哪个翻译服务，也可以不使用。你可以在 Scholia 的设置中退出 ChatGPT、删除 API 密钥、清除保存的译文，也可以随时删除你的文库文件夹。如需停止 iCloud 同步，可以在 Scholia 中关闭，或把文库移出 iCloud。

儿童

Scholia 并非面向儿童，也不会收集任何人的个人信息。

变更
